## 🎯 Objectives & Results

The Job That Starts with Objective and Key Results - Sets goals and objectives, aligning tasks with measurable results. Aim at it

## 🎯 Objectives

- **Clear Goals**: Define specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) objectives.
- **Alignment**: Ensure objectives align with the overall vision and strategy of the project or organization.
- **Key Results**: Identify key metrics or outcomes that will indicate progress towards the objectives.

## 🎯 Results

- **Measurable Outcomes**: Track and measure the progress towards the objectives using key results.
- **Feedback Loop**: Continuously monitor and adjust the objectives and key results based on feedback and performance data.
- **Achievement**: Celebrate and recognize the achievement of objectives and key results, fostering a culture of success and continuous improvement.
